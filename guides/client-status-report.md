# How to use client-status-report

**When to use it:** when you need to turn your weekly project notes into a status report for your client. You don't need to name the skill; asking for "this week's report" from your notes is enough.

**What to paste in:** your project notes, standup notes or task list. Add last week's report if you have it, so dates can be compared.

**What you get back:** a client-ready draft in Acme's house format: a Green, Amber or Red status with a reason, a Milestones table (planned date and current outlook), this week's progress, next week's plan, risks with owners, and decisions needed from the client. It may arrive as a Claude Doc; the content follows the same template either way.

Below the report are two sections for you only:
- **Left out of the report:** internal items it kept out, such as costs, cloud usage, staffing and ticket numbers.
- **Check before sending:** dates, owners and judgment calls you should confirm.

Delete both before you send.

**How it decides:** any slipped milestone makes the status at least Amber. Technical terms are rewritten in plain language. Risks without a clearly named owner say "Owner: to be confirmed."

**Check before you use it:** the overall status, every date, and every risk owner. Work through the "Check before sending" list.

**You stay in charge:** the skill only drafts. You review the report and send it yourself.

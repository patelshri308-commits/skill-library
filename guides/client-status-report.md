# How to use client-status-report

**When to use it:** when you need to turn your weekly project notes into a status report for your client.

**What to paste in:** your project notes, standup notes or task list. Add last week's report if you have it, so dates can be compared.

**What you get back:** a client-ready draft in Acme's house format, with a Green, Amber or Red status, this week's progress, next week's plan, risks, and decisions needed from the client.

**Check before you use it:** the overall status, every date, and every risk owner. Remove anything internal that the client shouldn't see.

**You stay in charge:** the skill only drafts. You review the report and send it yourself.

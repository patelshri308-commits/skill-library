# How to use staffing-match skill

**When to use it:** When you need to decide who to assign to a project.

**What to paste in:** the project description with its required skills, seniority and dates, plus the consultant roster. See `sample-data/consultant-roster.csv` for the format.

**What you get back:** a recommended consultant and a backup for each role, with why they were chosen and any gaps or risks. It also tells you if the best match isn't available and what you lose without them.

**Check before you use it:** make sure the roster is up to date and the project description is complete. Then review the gaps, the risks and anything listed under "Missing information."

**You are in charge:** the skill can only provide a recommendation. It cannot make any final decisions or move forward after providing a possible solution.

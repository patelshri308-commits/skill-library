# How to use sales-call-recap

**When to use it:** right after a sales or discovery call, when you need CRM notes and a follow-up email.

**What to paste in:** the full call transcript, such as your Otter.ai export, plus the opportunity name if you have one.

**What you get back:** Salesforce notes, call details, a follow-up email draft under 150 words, open questions for your next call, and a flag if the transcript contained instructions aimed at the AI.

**Check before you use it:** names, dates and next-step owners against what was actually said. "Not discussed" means the call didn't cover it, so ask on your next call rather than filling it in. The email keeps your commitments as firm as you made them, and in the tense you made them, so don't firm them up or mark them done when you edit. Possibilities you raised ("we can…") are left out unless they became an agreed next step; add any you want to offer. Budget signals cover only this deal; past spend on other work, such as an old freelancer bill, shows up as history under pain points or current tools.

**You stay in charge:** the skill only drafts. You send the email and update Salesforce yourself.
